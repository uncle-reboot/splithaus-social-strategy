# SplitHaus — "The Houses" : organic social strategy v2
2026-09-25. Builds on SPLITHAUS_ORGANIC_STRATEGY.md, incorporating the Dan / Wells thread:
older audience, playful, heartstrings + nostalgia, regional series, a series per character, static content layered in, characters not fake humans, build IP. No ads.

## 1. Who we are online
**A storybook universe of family houses, each one narrating the family that shares it.**
Playful on the surface, nostalgic underneath: "more time at the place you love."
SplitHaus is the studio behind it (bio, end-of-series moments, the practical posts), never the star.

Rules (non-negotiable):
- Characters are clearly stylized puppets. No photoreal AI people, no AI spokesperson. Cheaper, IG is cracking down on undisclosed AI people, audiences distrust fake humans, and it gives us ownable IP.
- Fiction is labeled ("a fictional family"). No fake testimonials, no fake follower submissions.
- Warm toward every character, including the difficult ones. We laugh with families, not at them.

## 2. The universe: one house per region
Each house = its own narrator voice, family cast, seasonal rhythm and look. Same felt-diorama style across all so it reads as one world.

| House | Region | Signature fight / feeling | Peak season |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Lake Cabin** (built 1956) | Northwoods / Great Lakes | Who gets the Fourth | Summer; fall closing-up |
| **The Mountain House** | Rockies / Vermont ski | Who gets Christmas week, powder days | Winter |
| **The Beach Cottage** | East Coast (Cape / Outer Banks / Jersey Shore) | Who gets the August weeks, sandy floors, the outdoor shower | Summer |
| Later: Gulf / West Coast / farmhouse | — | Add only once one of the first three is working | — |

Launch with the Lake Cabin (assets exist), add the Mountain House for ski season (Nov–Dec), Beach Cottage for summer-planning season (Feb–Apr).

Other houses get their own versions of these archetypes (the ski-house organizer, the beach-house Dave), which makes regional series cheap to extend and relatable everywhere.

## 4. The content mix
Weekly, IG home base + cross-post FB (older audience lives there):
- **2 episode Reels** (rotating character series). 20–35s, end on a question ("Who's the Dave in your family?"), not a logo.
- **2 static posts** (near-free): character quote cards, "House Rules" illustrated carousels, nostalgia prompts ("the smell of the cabin the first night of summer"), regional postcards of each house.
- **1 community post:** real follower house rules / guestbook memories (with permission), redrawn in the house style.
- **Stories daily:** polls ("Lake, mountain or beach?"), "which character are you", questions.
- **1 in ~8 posts** is practical/product (Ruth's Rules + "how fair families do it").

Emotional ratio: roughly half playful, half heartstrings. The heartstring posts are the ones that get sent to family chats (IG's top ranking signal is sends).

Heartstring story bank: closing the cabin for winter · the height marks on the door frame · the first grandchild's first swim · the summer after Grandpa · the last summer before a sibling wants to sell · the guestbook entries from 1978 · everyone there at once for June's 90th.

## 5. Showing up (how we post and talk)
- **Voice:** the House speaks in first person in captions and replies. Replies are warm, short, curious ("Tell me about your dock").
- **Consistency:** same style, same voices, same day/time slots. Each character series on a fixed weekday so people come back for it.
- **Community over broadcast:** ask for rules and memories every week; feature them. Participate (not link-drop) in cabin / lake / ski-house Facebook groups and Reddit.
- **Collabs:** co-author posts with lake-life, ski-town, beach-house and cottage-interior accounts. Regional houses make this natural (Beach Cottage × an Outer Banks account).
- **Owned list:** free "Family House Rules" template + short newsletter ("letters from the House").
- **Platforms:** Instagram + Facebook primary; YouTube Shorts free cross-post; TikTok for the Cousins series; Pinterest for static rules / checklists; X light (clips + House one-liners).

## 6. IP (protect what we're building)
- Keep a character bible: names, backstories, voices, visual reference sheets, house histories.
- Human-written scripts, human creative direction and character design decisions strengthen ownership. Purely AI-generated imagery on its own has limited US copyright protection, so the protectable assets are the scripts, characters-as-developed, and names.
- Consider trademarking the series name(s) once one proves itself.
- Keep one consistent voice per house (ElevenLabs paid plan, or a hired voice actor for the flagship House).

## 7. Cost to run (cheap pipeline)
- Stills: ~$0.02–0.07/image (x.ai). Static posts: <$0.50 each.
- Episodes: mostly free pan/zoom on stills + 2–3 animated shots on Grok 1.5 (~$0.70 each), Seedance only for hero shots. ~$2–5/episode.
- Voice: ElevenLabs Starter ~$5/mo (needed for commercial use). Music: licensed library or ElevenLabs paid.
- Rough total: **~$50–100/month** of generation + tools at 2 episodes/week. The real cost is human time: ~2 hrs/wk review + ~20 min/day community.

## 8. Measure (following, not ads)
Sends per reach · follows per 1K non-follower reach · saves (static) · submissions/week · newsletter sign-ups · SplitHaus sign-ups from bio (lagging). Judge after 90 days of consistent posting; monthly, double down on the best character series and cut the weakest.
